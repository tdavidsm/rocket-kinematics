How to play

Three challenges, one per kinematics equation with acceleration. In each, set your rocket's initial velocity vᵢ and acceleration a, then launch.

  1. Timed Burn — v_f = v_i + a·t. Given the burn time and a target final speed.
  2. Speed Gate — v_f² = v_i² + 2·a·d. Given a distance and the target speed to be going at it. (Decelerate too hard and you'll stall before the gate!)
  3. Target Landing — d = v_i·t + ½·a·t². Given a time and a target distance to land on.
  • Best of 3: every challenge is a match. Each round has fresh numbers; beat both bots to win the round, and win 2 rounds to clear the challenge and earn its ★.
  • Unlock order: clear a challenge to unlock the next.
  • Bots play at a random 75–90% of optimal — good, but beatable with sharp math.

Rules: acceleration can be forward (+) or reverse (−) but its size is at least 0.5 m/s² (steps of 0.1); initial velocity moves in steps of 0.5 m/s. The little sketches show the shape of your x-t, v-t and a-t graphs — start/end and curve, not exact readings.
