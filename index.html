How to play

Three challenges, one for each kinematics equation with acceleration. In every one you set your rocket's initial velocity vᵢ and acceleration a, then launch.

  1. Timed Burn — v_f = v_i + a·t. You're given the burn time and a target final speed.
  2. Speed Gate — v_f² = v_i² + 2·a·d. You're given a distance and the target speed to be going when you reach it.
  3. Target Landing — d = v_i·t + ½·a·t². You're given a time and a target distance to land on.

Because there are two unknowns and one equation, you get to choose one value and calculate the other. 🧪 Test flight flies your rocket alone so you can gather data; 🚀 Race the bots runs you against two computer rockets. Get closer to the target than both bots to clear the challenge and earn the medal. The bots are good (75–90% of perfect) but beatable — if your math is sharp.

Rules: acceleration is always forward and at least 0.5 m/s² (steps of 0.1); initial velocity moves in steps of 0.5 m/s.
